Connection & background operation

Bluetooth needs HTTPS and a compatible browser, such as Chrome or Edge on desktop or Chrome on Android. Connect one output device and, optionally, Civet. Reconnection restores the link at zero; press Start to begin again.

A Worker drives the 100 ms clock. Tab switching does not deliberately pause the session, but browser or operating-system suspension cannot be prevented. A missed control heartbeat or stale pressure sample faults the session. Keep the device’s physical stop within reach, especially for cloud control; network delays can delay a stop command.
